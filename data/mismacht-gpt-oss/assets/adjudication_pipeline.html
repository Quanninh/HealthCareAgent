  • Dual Hash Index: Sub-microsecond annotated_map ($O(1)$, 84.4 ns) & annotated_by_case.

2. Tripartite Engine & Judicial Routing

  • Group 3 (8.5%): Genuine divergence (50 cases) routed to Claude Sonnet 5.5 & DeepSeek-R1.

3. Batch Orchestration & Outputs

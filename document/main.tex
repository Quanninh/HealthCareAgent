\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This technical report investigates the design, implementation, and evaluation of Small Multimodal Models (SMMs) fine-tuned and augmented for clinical diagnosis in resource-constrained environments. We examine techniques including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation (QLoRA), and multi-agent coordination architectures to assist healthcare providers in diagnosing tropical and infectious diseases.
\end{abstract}

\vspace{1cm}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Small Language Models, Multimodal AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), QLoRA, Multi-Agent Systems, Tropical Diseases, Infectious Diseases, Clinical Decision Support.

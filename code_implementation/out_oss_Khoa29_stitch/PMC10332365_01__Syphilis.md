The patient presents with a pruritic rash on the extremities, characterized by pale-red macules and papules with clear borders, not fused, and a distribution limited to the trunk and extremities while sparing the palms, soles, and oral mucosa. On physical examination, many pale red macules and papules (2-5 mm in diameter) with clear borders without fusion were found on the extremities. The rash was mildly atrophic in the middle, with brown ring-like elevations around the edges and a few adherent scales. This morphology is classic for porokeratosis, a condition characterized by the formation of a conical plate of hyperkeratosis, which is a hallmark feature of this disease.

The distribution of the rash, limited to the trunk and extremities while sparing the palms, soles, and oral mucosa, helps to exclude other conditions such as palmoplantar pustulosis, hand-foot disease, and mucosal involvement seen in some lichenoid disorders. The rash did not involve the palms of the hands, soles of the feet or the oral mucosa.

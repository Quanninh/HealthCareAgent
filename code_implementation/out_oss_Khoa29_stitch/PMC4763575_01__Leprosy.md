Upon presentation, a 38-year-old man with known retroviral infection and disseminated tuberculosis, on antitubercular therapy and antiretroviral therapy for 5 months, presented with a 2-week history of weakness and numbness of the right hand. He noticed an edematous reddish raised lesion on the inner aspect of the right hand, which he attributed to a topical agent. He reported pain over the right elbow and forearm. Dermatologic examination revealed a single well-defined erythematous plaque, 20 × 5 cm, with raised borders on the ulnar aspect of the right hand and distal forearm, extending to the palm. A satellite lesion with erythema and edema was present on the upper forearm. All modalities of sensation were absent in these lesions. Both ulnar nerves were thickened, with tenderness on the right side.

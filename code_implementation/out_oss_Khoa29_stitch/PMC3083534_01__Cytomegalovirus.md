The primary consideration in this case is secondary hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis due to cytopenias, high ferritin, and hemophagocytosis in marrow and nodes. Blood examination showed significant cytopenias, with hemoglobin 6.4 g/dL, platelets 40,000/mm³, and a total leukocyte count of 2,500/mm³. The ferritin level was elevated at 690 µg/L, indicating a possible underlying inflammatory process. Furthermore, bone marrow aspiration showed hypocellularity with decreased granulopoiesis and erythropoiesis due to bone marrow suppression by CMV.

However, Langerhans cell histiocytosis is considered as a differential diagnosis, but it is excluded because nuclear grooves/lobulation were absent. A differential diagnosis of Langerhan's cell histiocytosis or histiocytosis X was considered; but lobulation and grooves in nuclei were not present.

Malignant histiocytosis is also considered, but it is deemed unlikely due to the low nuclear-to-cytoplasmic ratio, rare mitoses, and lack of prominent nucleoli. The cells in hemophagocytic syndrome have a low nucleocytoplasmic ratio and abundant lightly staining cytoplasm, and nucleoli are not prominent and mitotic figures are rare.

Infectious etiologies other than CMV are ruled out by negative cultures and serologies. Microscopy and culture for mycobacteria and fungi were negative, and other serological tests for Epstein-Barr virus (EBV), human herpes virus, and parvovirus were found to be negative.

CMV infection is identified as a trigger, not the primary diagnosis, because viral replication was confirmed but does not fully explain the pancytopenia and histiocytic engulfment. CMV IgM antibody detected was positive in the child denoting recent infection which was later confirmed by PCR, and bone marrow aspiration showed bone marrow suppression by CMV.

Considering these findings, the primary consideration of secondary hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis remains the most likely diagnosis, given the cytopenias, high ferritin, and hemophagocytosis in marrow and nodes. However, the diagnosis of hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis is outside the scope of this case presentation.

His systemic therapy consisted of subcutaneous methotrexate (25 mg weekly) for nine years and adalimumab (40 mg every other week) for seven years; topical regimens included halometasone + triclosan cream and calcipotriol + betamethasone dipropionate foam in the weeks preceding admission. He was obese (BMI 39 kg/m²).

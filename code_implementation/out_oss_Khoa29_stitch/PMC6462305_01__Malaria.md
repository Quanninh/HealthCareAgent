A space-occupying lesion (SOL) with raised intracranial pressure is contemplated because of the patient's progressive neurological decline. Following further review of the patient's clinical features, investigation results, and MRI brain, the diagnosis is modified to Primary Angiitis of the CNS (PACNS).

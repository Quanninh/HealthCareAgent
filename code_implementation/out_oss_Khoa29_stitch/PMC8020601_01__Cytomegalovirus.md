However, four days into treatment, the patient's condition worsened: oxygen requirement increased to 15 L/min, inflammatory markers rose, and a repeat CT showed progression to 60% lung involvement. Lopinavir/ritonavir and subcutaneous anakinra were added, after which his respiratory status improved with apyrexia and decreasing oxygen needs. This suggests that the patient's condition may be more complex than initially thought, and that additional factors may be contributing to his illness.

This suggests that the patient's symptoms may be related to an infectious or immunological process rather than an inflammatory bowel disease.

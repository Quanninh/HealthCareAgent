The patient tested positive for severe acute respiratory syndrome-coronavirus-2 (SARS-CoV-2), which explained her systemic symptoms. However, this did not account for her worsening headache, and the lack of neurologic signs made primary viral encephalitis less likely. This led us to consider other potential causes of her symptoms.

Given her history of immunosuppressive therapy with cyclophosphamide and prednisone, we considered the possibility of an opportunistic fungal infection. Further examination revealed her immune system compromised. This predisposition to opportunistic infections made cryptococcal meningitis a potential consideration.

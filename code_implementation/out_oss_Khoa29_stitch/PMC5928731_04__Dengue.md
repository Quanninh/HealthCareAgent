The patient presented with a nonspecific febrile illness, which led to an initial presumption of viral syndrome. He was given acetaminophen and discharged from the ED with a diagnosis of viral syndrome. However, given the geographic context and febrile illness, leptospirosis was considered a possibility. Serum collected on day 2 of illness was negative by PCR and MAT, making it less likely at presentation.

Despite the initial negative PCR, dengue infection remained a possibility. Definitive diagnosis required serologic confirmation, which was obtained later. Convalescent serum, collected at a later time, was also positive for detection of anti-dengue virus IgM antibodies. This positive serologic confirmation ultimately led to the diagnosis of dengue.

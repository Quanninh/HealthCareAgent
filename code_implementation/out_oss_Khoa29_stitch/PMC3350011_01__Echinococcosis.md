The initial leading diagnosis was a hydatid cyst due to imaging showing a unilocular cyst in the right hepatic lobe, a typical site for hepatic echinococcosis. Contrast computerized tomographic scan of the abdomen was done which revealed a unilocular cyst in the right lobe of liver with a provisional diagnosis of hydatid cyst liver.

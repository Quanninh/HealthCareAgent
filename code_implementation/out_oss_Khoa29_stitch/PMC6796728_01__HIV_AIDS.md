Given the patient's severe immunosuppression, we also entertained the possibility of mycobacterial infection, such as tuberculosis, as a differential. The differentials included mycobacterial infections, fungal infections, neoplastic disease, or Kaposi sarcoma.

Furthermore, Kaposi sarcoma was considered given the patient's HIV status, but lack of vascular tumor histology excluded it.

Therefore, bowel perforation due to tuberculosis was confirmed as the final diagnosis.

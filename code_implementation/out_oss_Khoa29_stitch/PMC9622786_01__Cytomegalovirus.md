Given the pulmonary nodules, tuberculosis is also considered, but it is excluded by negative PCR and negative acid-fast staining, as tuberculosis-polymerase chain reaction was negative; and staining of mycobacteria and fungi were negative.

Another consideration is secondary lung involvement by nodal/mediastinal diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), given the mediastinal lymphadenopathy. Given the repeated detection of EBV in the BAL NGS, an EBV-driven lymphoproliferative disorder is entertained. The patient's clinical presentation, including the presence of polymorphic lymphoid cells and the histopathology findings, further support this diagnosis. Immunohistochemical staining was positive for EBER and (60%) Ki-67, and the patient was diagnosed with lymphomatoid granulomatosis (grade 3).
